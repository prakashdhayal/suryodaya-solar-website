The central government's PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana pays part of the cost of rooftop solar on homes into the
household's bank account - up to ₹78,000 for a system of 3 kW or more.

Our guide explains who can apply, the steps on the national portal for a house in Ajmer, and what to keep ready.
