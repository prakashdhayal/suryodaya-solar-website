## What the scheme is

**PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** is the Government of India's scheme for rooftop solar on homes, started in
February 2024. A household puts solar panels on its own roof, connected to the grid with a net meter, and the central
government pays part of the cost as a subsidy (it calls it *central financial assistance*) straight into the household's
bank account.

## How much the subsidy is

The central subsidy, as published for the scheme:

| System size | Central subsidy |
|---|---|
| 1 kW | ₹30,000 |
| 2 kW | ₹60,000 |
| 3 kW or more | ₹78,000 (the most a home can get) |

That is ₹30,000 a kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third. The amount that applies to you is the one the
national portal shows when you apply.

## Until when

The scheme was approved to run until **March 2027** (the 2026-27 financial year). Any change to that, or a last date for
new applications, is announced on [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) - we will also post it in our
[news](/news/).

## Who can apply

- A household with **its own house** and a roof that gets sun for most of the day.
- A **valid electricity connection** for the house - in Ajmer, with AVVNL (Ajmer Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited).
- A house that has **not already had a subsidy** for rooftop solar.

## The steps, for a house in Ajmer

1. **Register** on [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/): choose Rajasthan and *Ajmer Vidyut Vitran Nigam
   Limited*, and enter your electricity consumer number (it is on your bill), mobile number and email.
2. **Log in and apply** for rooftop solar.
3. **Wait for AVVNL's approval** (the feasibility approval) on the portal.
4. **Get the system installed** by a vendor registered with AVVNL on the portal - the portal lists them.
5. **Submit the system's details** on the portal and apply for the net meter.
6. **AVVNL inspects** the system and fits the net meter; the portal then shows the **commissioning certificate**.
7. **Enter your bank account details** and a cancelled cheque on the portal.
8. **The subsidy is paid** into that bank account.

## What to keep ready

- Your latest **electricity bill** (for the consumer number).
- **Bank account** details and a cancelled cheque, in the name of the person applying.
- A **mobile number and email** you use - the portal sends its updates there.
